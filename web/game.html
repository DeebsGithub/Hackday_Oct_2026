    How to play

    Guess the chance today's mystery flight gets cancelled, to two decimals, in six tries.

    • Type a percentage like 4.21. Each guess unlocks another clue about the flight.
    • Digits work like Wordle: 4 right digit, right spot. 2 in the answer, wrong spot. 7 not in the answer.
    • The arrow tells you whether the real number is higher or lower.

    Odds come from the same model as the flight checker: historical rates by airline, route, season and hour, moved by live weather markets.
